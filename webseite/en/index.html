Why Puck Racer?

⚙ Affordable & DIY

Complete hardware under 20 € per puck. Hardware and software are fully open source – GitHub.

⚡ Professional Training

Over 27 games, detailed statistics, training plans and CSV export. Developed with PE teachers and coaches.

🔒 Private & Independent

No subscription, no cloud, no app. Runs in any browser on any device. Your data stays with you.

📡 ESP-NOW Radio Technology

Over 50 m range, automatic channel selection and reconnect. No internet needed – the pucks communicate directly.

🌐 7 Languages

The interface is available in German, English, French, Spanish, Italian, Chinese, ....

🔋 4+ Hours Battery

Rechargeable 18650 battery with intelligent protection circuit. OTA updates for coordinator and pucks via the web interface.
