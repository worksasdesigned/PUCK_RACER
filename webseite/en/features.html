Comprehensive Features

More than just a game – a complete training platform.

Games & Training Modes

Over 27 Unique Games

  • Variety: Covers reaction, cognition, endurance, speed and coordination.
  • Classics: Includes popular modes like reaction tests, Whac-a-Mole, Memory and Simon Says.
  • Sport-specific: Offers specialized training formats like Shuttle Run or Interval Timer (Tabata).

Flexible Game Modes

  • Single Player: Perfect your own skills and chase the high score.
  • Multiplayer: Compete directly against friends or teammates.
  • Team Mode: Play in color-coded teams against each other.
  • Parallel Arenas: Multiple game groups can train simultaneously and independently.

Customizable Rules

  • Parameters: Each game can be individually configured (duration, rounds, difficulty, etc.).
  • Player Selection: Choose exactly which players participate in a round.
  • Puck Selection: Define which pucks should be active for the current game.

Analysis & Planning

Professional Analysis

  • Live Statistics: Track round times and scores in real time during the game.
  • Player Statistics: Analyze individual player performance over time.
  • Game Statistics: Detailed evaluation after each completed game.
  • CSV Export: Export all relevant data for further processing in programs like Excel.

Comprehensive Planning

  • Player Manager: Create player profiles and manage them centrally.
  • Training Planner: Create and save complex training sequences from multiple games.
  • Multilingual: The entire interface is available in 7 languages and expandable.
  • Translation Editor: Easily customize terms or entire languages yourself.

Backup & System

  • Local Backup: Save all players, plans and settings in a single file.
  • Restore: Simply import a backup to load the previous state.
  • System Information: Keep track of storage space, uptime and firmware version.

Technology & Hardware

Self-contained Puck Hardware

  • Long Battery Life: Over 4 hours of battery life in continuous operation.
  • Bright LEDs: 35 individually controllable WS2812B LEDs per puck, clearly visible even in daylight.
  • Robust & Reliable: Sturdy arcade button and buzzer for acoustic feedback.
  • DIY-friendly: Open-source hardware and free 3D-printable adapters.

Smart Wireless Technology

  • ESP-NOW Protocol: Extremely fast and direct communication without a Wi-Fi router.
  • Long Range: Stable connection over 50 meters.
  • Auto-Reconnect: Pucks automatically reconnect after a restart or connection loss.
  • Channel Scan: The Coordinator checks for the best radio channel at startup to avoid interference.

Intelligent System

  • No App Required: Runs on any device with a modern web browser (phone, tablet, laptop).
  • Offline-First: No internet, no cloud, no data collection. Your data belongs to you.
  • OTA Updates: Conveniently update the firmware of the Coordinator and all pucks via the web interface.
  • Battery Protection: Intelligent monitoring and shutdown to protect the batteries.
